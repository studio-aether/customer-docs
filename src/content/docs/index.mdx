Studio Aether makes RedM scripts, creatures and systems for fantasy and medieval roleplay servers.
Pick a resource from the sidebar to see its installation guide, configuration reference and
troubleshooting. Buy on the [Studio Aether store](https://studio-aether.tebex.io).
