## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Six magic weapons as usable items: Frost Dagger, Fire Sword, Lifesteal Dagger, Poison Dagger, Frost Bow and Fire Bow.
- Effects on hit: slow, burning, lifesteal with a blood stream to the wielder, poison damage over time.
- Weapon auras on the drawn weapon, visible to nearby players.
- Magic bows shoot the player's own arrows from the VORP inventory.
- Hits validated on the server; kill credit stays with the wielder.
- Items are created in the database on first start.
- Languages: English, German, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Thai.
