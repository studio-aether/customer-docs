`aether_orcs` adds three orcs and one ogre to your server as ready-made peds. The models
are custom, delivered as YDD and YTD on the vanilla skeleton. The pack has no scripts: you
spawn the peds from your own code, or connect them to `aether_goblins` and get ambush
points, a spawn menu and combat without writing anything.

## The peds

| Model | Outfit | Scale | Height | Suggested weapon |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `aether_orc_grimfang` | 0 | 1.00 | 1.94 m | machete |
| `aether_orc_ironhide` | 1 | 1.04 | 2.02 m | hatchet |
| `aether_orc_ironjaw` | 2 | 1.02 | 1.98 m | cleaver |
| `aether_ogre_grimhide` | 3 | 1.3627 | 2.65 m | fists |

## Features

- **Three orcs and an ogre**, each with its own model and textures.
- **Ped models only**: no scripts, no framework, no database.
- **Vanilla skeleton**, so every game animation plays.
- **Spawn guide** with a copy-ready client function for your own scripts.
- **Ready entries for `aether_goblins`**: paste them into its config and the orcs and the ogre join its ambush points and spawn menu.

## Good to know

The first install needs a full server restart, a resource restart is not enough.
The pack replaces clothing of the vanilla ped `cs_mp_camp_cook`. If the game spawns that
ped, he wears orc gear. The ogre is large, and doorways and low interiors do not fit him.
