## 1.0.0 (2026-10-27)

- First release.
- One portal model with a spinning rune ring, glowing runes, vortex effect and light.
- Ring spins up when switched on and slows down into its rest position when switched off.
- Teleport with screen fade, also on horseback.
- Use modes per portal: hold-E prompt, walk in, or export only.
- Admin commands to switch, place, retarget and remove portals in game.
- Server exports to add, remove and switch portals, client exports to trigger a portal and read its state, and an event on every use.
